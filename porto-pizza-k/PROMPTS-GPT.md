# Prompts GPT · images du simulateur Porto Pizza K

À coller dans ChatGPT, **dans une seule conversation**, une image à la fois, dans l'ordre.
Pour chaque image, demander le **format carré 1:1, en haute résolution**, puis télécharger le fichier
et le renommer comme indiqué (ex. `tomate-crue.png`).

---

## Message 1 · le cadre (à envoyer en premier)

```
I need 10 ultra-photorealistic food photos for an interactive pizza builder app. They must look like
real professional food photography shot with a full-frame camera and a 100mm macro lens, NOT like
illustrations, 3D renders or AI art. Real textures, real imperfections, natural colors, no plastic
shine, no oversaturation, no cartoon look, no text, no logo, no hands, no props, no garnish.

Rules for ALL images:
- Square 1:1 format, highest resolution.
- Camera exactly top-down (90°, perfectly overhead), no perspective.
- Same soft natural daylight from the top-left on every image, so they all match together.
- Same scale: a pizza is about 30 cm wide; ingredient pieces must be at their real size
  relative to that pizza.

I'll ask for the images one by one. Reply "OK" and wait.
```

## Si une image ne va pas

- Trop « dessin » ou trop lisse : *« More photorealistic: real food photography, natural
  imperfections, less smooth, less saturated. »*
- La version cuite ne correspond pas à la crue : *« Keep exactly the same framing and the same
  topping positions as the previous image, only change the cooking. »*
- Des morceaux se touchent ou ont une ombre : *« Same image but with more space between pieces and
  absolutely no shadows. »*
